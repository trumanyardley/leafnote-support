How to get help

When contacting support, it helps to include:

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • A short description of the issue
  • Screenshots, if relevant

Feature requests and feedback

Suggestions for improving Leafnote are welcome. You can use the same support email for general feedback and feature requests.

About Leafnote
